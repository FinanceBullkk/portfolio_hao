The Evolution

Replacing fragmented scripts with a zero-friction platform

In early iterations, issuing 300+ certificates required juggling multiple disjointed tools: Canva for bulk design, online splitters for single-page files, local Python desktop scripts for renaming, Google Drive for hosting, and Google Sheets Apps Script for email dispatching. It took 3 to 5 days per batch, required technical oversight, and created risks of page-order misalignment bugs.

I architected CertStudio to consolidate the entire lifecycle into a single, high-performance web platform that non-technical event coordinators can run end-to-end without developer assistance.

Automated · OCR Extraction & Sheet-Driven Matching
Vietnamese OCR & Diacritic Normalization

The core safety guarantee: Every page reads its own text layer. pdfplumber extracts student names and certificate serials printed directly on the certificate. The engine applies Unicode diacritic normalization to match against uploaded Excel rosters, eliminating order-dependent misalignment risks.

UI / UX · Desktop-Grade Studio Workspace
Zero-Scroll Review & Click-to-Zoom Lightbox

Designed for high-speed verification: The application viewport is strictly locked at 100vh with zero page scrolling. 4
Automated · Pre-Flight Audit & High-Volume Delivery
Pre-Flight Safety Check & SMTP Dispatch

Before sending, a **Pre-Flight Safety Audit** validates recipient email syntax and flags missing addresses. Organizers can run a **Simulation (Dry-Run)** or dispatch via live Gmail/Outlook SMTP with automated throttling and real-time execution logs. Finished batches can be exported as a bundled **ZIP archive** or **Excel CSV delivery report** in 1 click.

Why it's solid

01
Every page names itself
The engine reads each page's own text layer. A re-sorted Sheet or a missing row cannot shift files out of alignment, preventing the silent disaster of sending someone else's certificate.
02
Safe to re-run (Idempotency)
Rate-limiting and idempotency tracking mean you can safely retry failed sends without double-emailing recipients or tripping Gmail SMTP quotas.
03
The team owns it
A drag-and-drop web UI, instant previews, and automated pre-flight checks mean event coordinators run the whole pipeline autonomously without a developer.
