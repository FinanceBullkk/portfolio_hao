Pillar 01 · Identity Governance & Security

Differentiator Anatomy

The directory sync is the part that can do real damage

A monthly sync touches every employee record at once. Done carelessly it locks people out of their own accounts, and IT absorbs the fallout. This is how the write path avoids that.

Under the hood

What makes it more than a booking tool

01
It keeps a learning record, not just bookings
Most booking tools forget the session once it happens. Here every employee's history, hours and credentials land in one profile.
02
Completion is measured, not judged
Three gates replace the instructor's opinion. Every certificate carries a verification code and an expiry date.
03
The sync cannot lock people out
Matching on empCode lets an update skip password hashing entirely for existing staff.
04
The export is treated as untrusted output
The HR Excel export escapes any cell starting with =, +, - or @, preventing formula injection.
