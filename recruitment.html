Recruitment Intake

Zapier · Notion

No-code Automation for Club Applications

About 40 club-membership applications arrived through a Google Form into a flat Sheet. I turned that into a Notion board the team could track and comment on. The whole recruiting team switched to it and kept using it.

Why it stuck

Adoption was the real win

01
From rows to records
In a flat sheet you cannot track one candidate or discuss them. As Notion records, every applicant became something you could open, assign and comment on.
02
No new habits required
Applicants still used the same Google Form. The automation ran behind it, so nobody had to learn a new tool to make the change work.
03
The team chose to keep it
Per-candidate review and commenting replaced a spreadsheet nobody could work in. The team adopted it on their own, which is the only real test of an internal tool.
